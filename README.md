# 🌱 Eco-Tech & Sustainability

### Building a Greener and More Sustainable Future

## About the Project

Eco-Tech & Sustainability is an educational website that explores environmental issues, sustainable practices, and the role of green technology in protecting our planet.

The website aims to raise environmental awareness by providing accessible information about sustainability and encouraging individuals to adopt more environmentally responsible habits.

## Objectives

- Raise awareness about major environmental issues.
- Introduce different forms of green technology.
- Explain the benefits and limitations of renewable energy.
- Encourage sustainable habits and responsible resource consumption.
- Promote environmental awareness and action.

## 🌍 Website Pages

### 1. Home (`index.html`)
Introduces the website and discusses important environmental issues, including:
- Climate Change
- Deforestation
- Plastic Pollution
- Green Technology
- Everyday Sustainable Solutions

### 2. Green Technology (`green-tech.html`)
Explores different renewable energy sources and sustainable technologies, including:
- Solar Energy
- Wind Energy
- Hydroelectric Energy
- Renewable Energy Comparisons

### 3. Action Plan (`action-plan.html`)
Encourages visitors to practice sustainability through:
- Carbon Footprint Checklist
- Electricity and Carbon Footprint Estimator
- Sustainable Action Plan
- Environmental Resources
- Educational Videos

## Technologies Used

- **HTML5** – Provides the structure and content of the website.
- **CSS3** – Handles the visual design, layout, and responsiveness.
- **JavaScript** – Adds interactive features and calculations.

## Key Features

- Educational environmental content
- Renewable energy comparisons
- Interactive checklists
- Carbon footprint estimation
- Sustainability tips and recommendations
- External environmental resources

Why sustainability matters

Devices, data centers, and electricity all use resources. Small, smart choices add up when millions of people make them.

Climate change

Burning fossil fuels for power releases greenhouse gases. Cleaner energy cuts these emissions.

E-waste

Old phones and computers contain metals that can be reused. Thrown away, they pollute soil and water.

Resource use

Making new devices uses water, minerals, and energy. Repairing and reusing saves all three.

Green technologies

These solutions already work today.

Energy

Solar and wind power

Produce electricity with no fuel and almost no emissions.

Efficiency

Smart grids and IoT

Sensors track power and water use so waste can be found and fixed.

Transport

Electric vehicles

Cut tailpipe pollution, especially when charged from clean power.

Circular

Recycling and repair

Reusing materials keeps devices out of landfills.

Digital

Green computing

Efficient code, cloud servers on renewable power, and sleep settings reduce energy use.

Water

Smart irrigation

Soil sensors water plants only when needed.
